Odia Picture Dictionary

The Odia Picture Dictionary by Abhaya is a free offline Odia dictionary and visual dictionary. Search any word in Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ), English or Hindi (हिंदी), see picture cards, and hear native pronunciation with text-to-speech. Also useful as a picture dictionary for kids and beginners.

Features include trilingual search, Odia-to-Odia meanings, offline document reader for PDF and text files, and private on-device storage after the first load. No signup and no ads.
